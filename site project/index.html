Z Truck Repair Website Plan

WDD 231 Individual Website Project

Site Name

Z Truck Repair

The name Z Truck Repair was selected because it clearly represents a professional truck repair and maintenance company. The name is short, easy to remember, and directly communicates the type of services the business provides.

Suggested Domain: www.ztruckrepair.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Z Truck Repair website is to provide customers with clear information about truck repair and maintenance services. Visitors will be able to learn about the company, explore available services, understand the benefits of preventive maintenance, and find contact information for requesting assistance.

The website will also provide useful information for truck owners and transportation businesses that depend on reliable commercial vehicles.

Target Audience Scenarios

The website is designed for truck owners, drivers, transportation businesses, and fleet managers who need dependable truck repair and maintenance services.

  • What truck repair services does Z Truck Repair provide?
  • How can I schedule a truck repair appointment?
  • Where is Z Truck Repair located?
  • What are the company's operating hours?
  • Does the company provide preventive maintenance?
  • Can I contact the business about spare parts?

Website Pages

  1. Home Page — Introduction to Z Truck Repair, company overview, hero image, and highlights of the main services.
  2. Services Page — Information about engine repair, brake and suspension repair, electrical diagnostics, preventive maintenance, and spare parts.
  3. Contact Page — Business location, telephone number, email address, business hours, contact form, and location information.

Reason for Choosing This Subject

I chose Z Truck Repair because I am interested in the automotive industry and businesses that support transportation. Truck repair services are important for keeping commercial vehicles safe, reliable, and efficient.

Transportation businesses depend on trucks to move goods and provide services, so regular maintenance and timely repairs are important. The Z Truck Repair website will help customers learn about available repair and maintenance services while providing an easy way to contact the business.

Planned JavaScript Features

The completed website will include JavaScript functionality to make the site dynamic and interactive.

  • Fetch API data for truck repair services.
  • Display at least fifteen dynamic data items.
  • Use local storage for customer preferences.
  • Use a modal dialog for additional information.
  • Use event listeners for user interactions.
  • Use array methods to process data.
  • Use template literals to generate dynamic HTML.
  • Use ES modules to organize JavaScript code.
  • Use try-catch for asynchronous operations.

Website Goals

  • Provide customers with clear information about truck repair services.
  • Create a professional and trustworthy online presence.
  • Make it easy for customers to contact the business.
  • Provide a responsive experience on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
  • Follow accessibility, usability, SEO, and web development standards.
