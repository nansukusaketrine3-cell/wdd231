Z Truck Repair Website Plan

WDD 231 Individual Website Project

Site Name

Z Truck Repair

The name Z Truck Repair was selected because it clearly represents a professional truck repair and maintenance company. It is short, easy to remember, and reflects the services offered.

Suggested Domain: www.ztruckrepair.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide customers with information about truck repair and maintenance services. Visitors will learn about the company, explore available services, find business contact information, and use the contact page to request assistance.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • What truck repair services does Z Truck Repair provide?
  • How can I schedule a repair appointment?
  • Where is the business located?
  • What are the company's operating hours?

Reason for Choosing This Subject

I chose Z Truck Repair because I am interested in the automotive industry and businesses that support transportation. Truck repair services are essential for keeping commercial vehicles safe, reliable, and efficient. This website will help customers learn about available repair and maintenance services while providing an easy way to contact the business.
